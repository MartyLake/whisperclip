  <action>
Write tests first, then the type. `SpeakerTimeline` is a `Sendable` struct with no FluidAudio types in
its public surface (it takes `[Float]` probabilities, a frame count and a speaker count):
- `append(probabilities:frameCount:numSpeakers:)` — extends the timeline; time is `frames * 0.01 s`.
- `coveredSeconds` — how far the timeline reaches.
- `turns(from:to:)` — contiguous single-speaker spans: per frame the highest slot above the activity
  threshold wins (D-08); same-speaker gaps shorter than a merge gap are bridged; turns shorter than a
  minimum are dropped. Threshold, merge gap and minimum are named constants in `Const.swift` style.
- `dominantSpeaker(from:to:)` — slot with the most active frames in the range, or `nil` for silence.
  This is the query Phase 13 will use.
- `label(forSlot:)` — "Speaker N" (D-04).
Memory: an hour of meeting is 360,000 frames x 8 slots. Store what the queries need, not raw `Float`s
forever — decide the representation and state its size per hour in a comment.

Tests cover: single speaker, alternating speakers, silence, overlap, a turn that spans two appended
chunks, gap merging, minimum-duration drop, range query partly beyond `coveredSeconds`, label stability.
  </action>
  <verify>
    <automated>swift test --filter SpeakerTimelineTests</automated>
  </verify>
  <done>Tests pass; the type has no dependency on FluidAudio.</done>
