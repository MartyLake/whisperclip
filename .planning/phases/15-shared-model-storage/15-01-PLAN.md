    blocked; throws a `LocalizedError` on timeout.
  - `var fluidAudioRoot: URL`, `var downloaderBase: URL` (root; `ModelDownloader` appends `models/`).
- Logging: counts and status only; paths only with `LOG_SENSITIVE_DATA=1`.
